# Funding and contributor rewards

Hekara is free and open source. This page explains how sponsorship money is used and how contributors can be paid, so everything is in the open.

## Where the money comes from

- **Sponsorship:** voluntary payments from individuals and organisations (see the [sponsor page](https://srinivas26k.github.io/Hekara/) or sponsor directly at https://rzp.io/rzp/G5kLjzP).
- **Paid services:** setup, support and custom work for organisations (see [COMMERCIAL.md](../COMMERCIAL.md)).

The maintainer is an individual in India. Sponsorship is income to the maintainer; it is not held in a separate legal entity or fund, and it is not tax-deductible for sponsors.

## Where it goes

| Share                           | Use                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **20% of sponsorship received** | The contributor bounty pool (below)                                                  |
| Remainder                       | Maintainer time, hosting for demos, and saving toward an independent security review |

Percentages apply to sponsorship only, not to fees for paid services. The maintainer may change them, and will update this page and announce it in Discussions.

## Bounties

A bounty is a fixed amount for a specific, well-described piece of work.

1. **Find one.** Issues with the [`bounty`](https://github.com/Srinivas26k/Hekara/labels/bounty) label show the amount in the issue. Only the maintainer adds bounties, and only when there is money in the pool to pay for them.
2. **Claim it.** Comment on the issue that you are taking it. One person per bounty at a time; if there is no progress after two weeks, it can be handed to someone else.
3. **Do the work** following [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md): tests, docs, the code rules, and a signed-off commit.
4. **Get it merged.** The bounty is for work that is **reviewed and merged**. Review can ask for changes; a pull request that never meets the bar is not paid.
5. **Get paid** within 14 days of the merge.

### Paying you

- **In India:** UPI or bank transfer.
- **Outside India:** only after the maintainer confirms a legal and affordable way to pay you. Cross-border payments have their own rules and fees. Ask before you start work, not after.
- You are responsible for any tax on what you receive in your country. The maintainer will keep a record of every payment.

### Terms

- No bounty is guaranteed, and the pool can run out. Do not do paid-for work you cannot afford to do unpaid.
- The maintainer decides whether work meets the bounty. If there is a disagreement, an honest, partial payment for partial work is possible but not owed.
- Your work is licensed under the AGPL-3.0, with the additional grant in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#licence-of-contributions). Being paid does not change who owns your copyright.
- Employees: get your employer's written permission before working for pay.
- Do not collude, copy others' work, or submit machine-generated work you have not understood and tested. Doing so ends your eligibility.

## Transparency

Each quarter the maintainer will post a short note in Discussions with the total sponsorship received, the amount added to the bounty pool, and the bounties paid. Sponsors and contributors are thanked by name unless they ask not to be.
