## About certifications

Being **HIPAA compliant**, **SOC 2 attested** or **ISO 27001 certified** is something an _organisation_ achieves, through its own policies, operations and an independent auditor. No software can hold those on its own, and **Hekara does not claim any certification.**

What can be offered is the technical foundation and the preparation work: software with the right safeguards built in, a deployment that matches your obligations, and help gathering evidence for your audit. Engagements are scoped honestly: where a certification requires work only your organisation can do (policies, training, vendor agreements, an auditor's opinion), we will say so up front.

## Commercial licensing, in plain terms

The AGPL lets anyone use and modify Hekara, but if you run a modified version as a network service you must offer its source to your users under the same licence. If that doesn't fit, for example you want to embed Hekara in a closed-source product, ask about a commercial licence. Contributors grant the maintainer the right to include their work in commercial editions; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#licence-of-contributions).
