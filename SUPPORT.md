# Getting help

## Free community support

- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Srinivas26k/Hekara/discussions). Search first; someone may have asked already.
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/Srinivas26k/Hekara/issues/new/choose) with the version or commit, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce.
- **Documentation:** the [docs](docs/) cover architecture, security, deployment and portability. The [README](README.md) has the quick start.

Please **never post real patient or staff information**. Use the synthetic demo data when you need an example or a screenshot.

This is a volunteer-run project, so replies are best-effort and there is no guaranteed response time.

## Security problems

Do not use public channels. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
