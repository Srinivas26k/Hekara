Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information is collected when you sponsor Hekara, buy a service, or contact me, and how it is used. The controller is Nampalli Srinivas, an individual in India.

What I collect

  • From Razorpay when you pay: your name, email address, phone number if you provide it, the amount, payment ID and status, payment method type, and country. I never see your full card number, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • When you email me or open an issue: your email address, name and what you write.
  • For a paid service: the contact and business details needed to agree and deliver it.

This website has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies and makes no third-party requests.

Why I use it

  • to confirm and thank you for a payment, and to deliver a service you bought;
  • to keep accounting and tax records that the law requires;
  • to respond to your questions, and to prevent fraud and abuse.

I do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your consent.

Who else sees it

  • Razorpay processes payments under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the project's code and discussions, under its privacy statement. Anything you post in an issue or discussion is public.
  • My accountant and tax authorities, where the law requires.

How long I keep it

Payment and invoice records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law in India requires. Emails and other correspondence are kept while they are useful and then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent, by emailing srinivasvarma764@gmail.com. I will respond within a reasonable time. Records I am legally required to keep cannot be deleted until the retention period ends. This policy is written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.

Grievances

For a complaint about how your data is handled, write to Nampalli Srinivas at srinivasvarma764@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy grievance".

Security and changes

I use reputable providers and avoid keeping more than I need. I may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
