Support Hekara

Hekara is free, open-source scheduling software for home health agencies. Sponsorship keeps it maintained, tested and documented, and lets contributors be paid for their work.

Sponsor with any amount

Pay by UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay. Choose the amount that suits you. Sponsorship is voluntary and does not buy a product or a guarantee (see the terms).

Sponsor with Razorpay

Payments are processed by Razorpay. Card and bank details never reach this site or the project.

Using Hekara? Please give back

Hekara is free to use and self-host, and always will be. It is also built and maintained by one independent developer, and every fix, security patch and review takes time. If Hekara runs part of your agency or product, please don't just clone it and leave: put a fair share back so it keeps improving.

You are A fair way to give back
An agency or clinic running it Sponsor monthly, even a small amount, or hire support for setup, hosting and training. It costs far less than per-seat software.
A company building a product on it Sponsor at a level that matches the value you get, fund a feature, or ask about a commercial licence.
A developer Fix a bug, send a pull request, or take a paid bounty.

Paying is not a licence requirement; the AGPL-3.0 gives you these freedoms. It is how an open-source project you depend on stays alive.

What sponsorship pays for

  • Maintainer time: fixing bugs, reviewing contributions, keeping the project secure and documented.
  • Contributors: a share of sponsorship funds bounties for merged work. The rules are public in docs/FUNDING.md.
  • Costs: hosting for demos and an eventual independent security review.

Paid support and custom work

Organisations can engage the maintainer for installation and hosting, upgrades and migrations, training, integrations, and compliance-readiness support. Scope and fees are agreed in writing before work starts, and invoiced in INR or USD. See COMMERCIAL.md for what is offered, and note that Hekara itself holds no HIPAA, SOC 2 or other certification.
