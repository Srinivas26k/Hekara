How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. They typically reach you within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer. Any payment-processor fees that Razorpay does not return may be deducted from partial refunds.

Delivery

All services are digital and delivered by email, video call, GitHub or a shared repository. Nothing is shipped. The start date and timeline are agreed in writing before you pay.

Contact

To cancel or ask for a refund, email srinivasvarma764@gmail.com with your name, the payment ID and the reason. I aim to reply within 2 business days.
